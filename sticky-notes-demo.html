🗒️ Sticky Notes Demo

Interactive sticky notes system for Ryori-Bon

📝 How to Use Sticky Notes

  • Create: Click the green + button (bottom-right) or use the demo buttons above
  • Move: Click and drag the note header to reposition
  • Resize: Drag the resize handle in the bottom-right corner of each note
  • Edit: Click inside the note area to type your content
  • Color: Use the dropdown in the header to change colors
  • Delete: Click the × button in the header
  • Persistence: Notes are automatically saved to your browser's local storage
