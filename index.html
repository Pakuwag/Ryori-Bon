📝 Sticky Notes

Use sticky notes to add personal reminders and comments throughout the app!

How to use:

  • Click the green + button in the bottom-right corner to create a new note
  • Drag notes around by clicking and holding the header area
  • Resize notes by dragging the handle in the bottom-right corner
  • Change note colors using the dropdown in the header
  • Delete notes by clicking the × button
  • Notes are automatically saved and will persist between sessions
